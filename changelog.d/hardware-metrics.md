role `hardware_metrics` (control hardware-metrics): a timer writes a host's hardware state as node-exporter textfile metrics, read-only: pool capacity, health and error counters; the board controller's sensors, fans, power supplies and critical event-log entries (in-band); per-GPU temperature, power, power limit, load and memory, the GPUs the driver can no longer reach, the driver's fault reports in the kernel log and the processes computing on each card; and the hypervisor's guests (running, start at boot). Module `prometheus`: a hardware rule group on those families and the host exporter's CPU and NVMe temperatures (thresholds and windows are settings, with a GPU power cap), opt-in SNMP polling through an exporter beside the service (interface state, errors and speed of watched ports; a storage appliance's disks, fans and volumes; the community by secret reference), and opt-in one-token completion probes of model roles through the LLM gateway (failure and slowness alerts; the key by secret reference).
