Registry service schema 2.13 (additive): a row declares the callbacks its own product serves for a sign-in at the identity provider with `oidc.redirect_uris`, on an `auth: idp` row. The IdP realm generator gives that row's client exactly those redirects, in addition to the public route's own; until now a row with no public hostname (a forge whose sign-in button lives at its own internal name) got a client with no redirect at all. The registry holds each URI to the row: one explicit https URL, no wildcard, query, fragment or userinfo, the host is the alias-zone name the row answers by (with the row's own port) or its `public_hostname`, and no dot segment, empty segment or encoded slash in the path, so the IdP is never told to send a sign-in to a service that is not that row. The generic rule (no public hostname, no redirect) is unchanged. Frozen copy `schemas/_frozen/2.13`, example and negative fixtures, pinned by `tests/schemas/test_service_2_13.py` and `tests/registry/test_service_oidc_redirects.py`.
