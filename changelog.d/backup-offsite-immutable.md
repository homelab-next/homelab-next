conformance suite `backup` (extended): an off-site immutable lane. Six new checks probe one property real labs need (ADR-0042 F-09): `copy-offsite-roundtrip`, `verify-offsite`, `delete-must-fail`, `shorten-retention-must-fail`, `disable-versioning-must-fail` and `restore-from-offsite`. Three of those are "must-fail" probes - they are not actions an operator wants to succeed, they are how the suite asks a subject to prove the credential the automation holds CANNOT delete an off-site copy, shorten its retention window or disable versioning. The lane rides on a new product feature `offsite-immutable`; subjects that do not declare it still run the nine on-site checks and skip the six. The fake `ObjectLockBackupStore` (`conformance/fakes/backup_store.py`) models an S3 bucket in Object Lock compliance mode and ships with five deliberately defective subjects - `DeletableOffsiteStore`, `ShortenableOffsiteStore`, `VersioningDisablableOffsiteStore`, `SilentOffsiteVerifyStore`, `LossyOffsiteRestoreStore` - each caught by its named check. Protocol `BackupStore` grows six methods and two exceptions (`OffsiteCopy`, `NoSuchOffsiteCopy`, `OffsiteImmutable`), all documented in the suite docstring.
