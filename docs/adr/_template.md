# ADR-NNNN: Title in the imperative

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN | rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Related:** other ADRs, schemas or docs
- **Depends-on:** ADR-NNNN, ... (optional: the ADRs this decision rests on; an accepted ADR may
  depend only on accepted ones)
- **Amends:** ADR-NNNN, ... (optional: the ADRs this one changes; each of them lists this one under
  **Amended-by**, and an **Amended:** line in prose names it too)

## Context

What forces are at play. Describe the shape of the evidence, never real identifiers.

## Decision

What we do, as numbered, testable statements.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what it obliges us to maintain.

## Alternatives

What else was considered and why it was not chosen.
