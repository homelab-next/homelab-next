## Question

Is anyone outside the lab probing or attacking its monitoring and status endpoints, which would blind the lab to an outage?

## Decision supported

Whether to restrict the monitoring endpoints further (a proposal) and whether [[01a15e4e-758d-75ed-a383-a78599b3d065]]'s answer may be under attack.

## Acceptance

A request burst against the status host from an address outside the lab's zones is reported as a finding within a day, naming the client and the window.

## Guardrails

Authorized internal testing of the operator's own lab only; findings stay internal and identifying details are redacted before any summary leaves the lab.
