## Purpose

## Steps

1. Create examples/registers/lessons/LL-NEW-<slug>.md with `kind` and the fields its kind needs (a pitfall's Symptom, Contributing factor and Root cause sections; a practice's Method and applies_to).

   - verify: python3 -m homelabnext.registers build examples/registers build/registers exits 0
