## Symptom

Every web UI behind the proxy showed a browser certificate warning at 06:10 UTC. Monitoring reported the proxy as up because it only checked the TCP port.

## Contributing factor

The renewal timer ran, but renewal failed silently because the DNS challenge credential had been rotated and the renewal job logged the error without a non-zero exit.

## Root cause

Nothing measured certificate lifetime from the client side, so a failed renewal was indistinguishable from a successful one until expiry.

## Preventive control

A daily check connects to every listener over TLS and fails when the certificate has fewer than 14 days left; the result pages through the invariants runner.
